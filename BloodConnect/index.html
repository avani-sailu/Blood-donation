About Our Mission

Connecting Hearts, Giving Life

Bridging emergency blood requirements with compassionate community donors.

Our Vision

No patient should suffer or lose a life waiting for blood. BloodConnect aims to create an omnipresent digital support system accessible anywhere across the country.

Our Mission

Empower citizens through technology to publish urgent blood requests, notify matching blood group donors in real-time, and streamline donor acceptance and fulfillment.

Why BloodConnect?

  • ✓
    Direct Request & Accept: Requesters publish requirements and donors directly accept.
  • ✓
    Location-Based Matching: Donors and requests mapped across cities and hospital zones.
  • ✓
    Built-in Chat & Coordination: Direct messaging between donor and patient family.
  • ✓
    End-to-End Status Tracking: From Open to In Progress, Donor Selected, and Completed.
Simple & Fast Workflow

How BloodConnect Works

Four simple steps from request publication to life-saving transfusion.

1

Register

Sign up as a donor or requester with your blood group and location details.

2

Create / Find Request

Post a blood requirement with patient details, hospital location, and urgency.

3

Accept a Request

Matching eligible donors accept requests and confirm their availability time.

4

Connect & Donate

Requester selects donor, coordinates via chat, and completes the donation.

Compatibility Guide

Blood Groups & Compatibility

Explore compatibility to understand which donors can donate to whom.

O- Universal Donor
Can donate red blood cells to anyone of any blood group in emergencies.
Can Give To: All Blood Types (Universal)
Can Receive: O- only
O+ Most Requested
Most common blood type; in constant high demand by hospitals.
Can Give To: O+, A+, B+, AB+
Can Receive: O+, O-
A+ Common
Very common blood type; essential for platelets and red cells.
Can Give To: A+, AB+
Can Receive: A+, A-, O+, O-
A- Rare
Can give red cells to both positive and negative A and AB patients.
Can Give To: A+, A-, AB+, AB-
Can Receive: A-, O-
B+ High Demand
Important for trauma and oncology treatments.
Can Give To: B+, AB+
Can Receive: B+, B-, O+, O-
B- Rare
One of the rarest blood groups. Every donor makes an enormous impact.
Can Give To: B+, B-, AB+, AB-
Can Receive: B-, O-
AB+ Universal Recipient
Can safely receive red blood cells from any blood type.
Can Give To: AB+ only
